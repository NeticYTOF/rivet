# Cores and rewards

**Cores** are the spendable currency. You earn them by shipping, and you spend them in the shop.

## How projects are rewarded

Projects earn Cores based on four things:

1. **Originality** — is this your work, or a clone of something that already exists?
2. **Technicality** — how deep does the technical work actually go?
3. **Execution** — does it work, and does it work well?
4. **Documentation** — how well did you document the build?

Better projects get higher multipliers. The same project quality scored across all four dimensions is worth more than a project that only clears one or two of them.

## Bonuses

On top of the base Cores, there are bonuses for projects that get real users, contributors, downloads, or adoption outside LOADOUT. If your tool ends up being used by people who are not in the program, that counts for something.

## AI rules

AI can help, but it should not build the project for you. The current draft position is a maximum of 40% AI-generated implementation. Ask a human maintainer if you are unsure how a specific project sits against that line — do not guess and do not quietly exceed it.

## Other planned systems

These are planned and not active yet: journals, seasons, track leaderboards, quests, sponsor drops, signal bonuses, and limited rewards. Treat any question about how they work as unanswered rather than guessing; the maintainers will confirm when they are built.
