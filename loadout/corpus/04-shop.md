# The shop

Cores buy equipment. What you buy is the Physical Loadout, and it exists so you can build the next, harder thing.

## What is in the shop

- Hosting and domains
- AI and GPU compute
- Raspberry Pis and FPGA boards
- Hacker tech
- RAM and SSDs
- PC upgrades
- GPUs
- 3D printers
- Mini PCs
- Laptops
- Travel grants

**The shop is not final.** Participants help decide what actually gets added to it.

## Shop suggestions

A good LOADOUT shop contains stuff people actually want. If you want something added, post it in the Shop Suggestions tab. If someone already suggested it, upvote it instead of duplicating it.

## Custom Orders

Once you have built enough, you unlock Custom Orders. If what you want is not already in the shop, you can request it yourself — a GPU, a laptop, a mini server, a weird dev board, a Steam Deck, an FPGA, or whatever other tech actually makes sense for the build you are doing next.

Custom Orders still require the required Builder Level and the required Cores. Unlocking Custom Orders does not mean anything is free; it means the catalogue stops being the limit on what you can ask for.
