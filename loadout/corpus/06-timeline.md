# Timeline and how to get involved

The timeline will definitely change. Treat it as direction, not as dates.

- **Now** — feedback and the website.
- **Next** — RSVP and a small test.
- **After** — sponsors and a pilot season.
- **Later** — full launch.

## Where to find people

- Slack: `#loadout`
- Website: loadout-jerry-team1.vercel.app
- RSVP: rsvp.soon.it/loadout

RSVP registers interest. It does not enroll you or accept you into the program.

## Who is building Loadout

Loadout is made by netic, wind, and jerry.

## Feedback the maintainers are asking for

- Are the tracks good?
- Would you actually care about levelling your Builder Profile?
- What would you really save Bolts for?
- Should levels unlock different rewards?
- What feels too similar to another YSWS?

Post ideas in `#loadout`.
