- **Jerry — Lead organizer.** Leads Loadout and develops its website.

- **Netic (Notios) — Developer and co-organizer.** Develops Rivet, Loadout's Slack bot, and helps organize the program.

Jerry is the lead org of Loadout.

## Community context

Hack Club is a nonprofit community of teenagers who learn by making and sharing their own projects. Its You Ship, We Ship programs encourage building by offering prizes for projects. Loadout is a YSWS proposal in development; Hack Club acceptance has not been confirmed yet.
