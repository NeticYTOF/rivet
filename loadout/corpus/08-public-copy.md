# Public copy

Tightened LOADOUT copy for Slack Canvas and the website. Section headings are bold and underlined; related sentences stay in the same block instead of each thought taking its own paragraph.

## Slack Canvas version

Slack renders bold and underline together as `*_text_*`.

```text
LOADOUT — a YSWS where every project levels you up as a builder.

Build projects, grow your Builder Profile, and use what you earn to upgrade your setup.

Status: early draft — gathering feedback, building the website, and looking for sponsors.

*_The idea_*
Most YSWSes reward the project you just made. LOADOUT rewards your progression as a builder. You ship projects across different technical tracks, you get track levels and Cores that unlock and buy better tech, and levels unlock it while Cores buy it. The higher you level, the cheaper shop items for your track become; every 3 levels you gain a reduction coupon for that track, with a maximum of 25% reduction.

*_Builder tracks_*
Compute — AI, GPUs, graphics, performance, inference.
Systems — servers, compilers, networking, infrastructure.
Hardware — PCBs, robotics, FPGA, embedded.
Build — apps, games, websites, creative tools.
Research — experiments, benchmarks, technical exploration.

Your profile might look like: Compute LV.7 · Systems LV.4 · Hardware LV.3. The more you build in one area, the more that track levels up.

*_What can I get?_*
The shop could include hosting and domains, AI and GPU compute, Raspberry Pis and FPGA boards, hacker tech, RAM and SSDs, PC upgrades, GPUs, 3D printers, mini PCs, laptops, and travel grants. The shop is not final — participants will help decide what actually gets added.

*_Custom orders_*
Once you build enough you unlock Custom Orders. If what you want isn't already in the shop, request it yourself: a GPU, laptop, mini server, weird dev board, Steam Deck, FPGA, or whatever other tech makes sense. You still need the required Builder Level and Cores.

*_How projects are rewarded_*
Projects earn Cores based on Originality, Technicality, Execution, and how well you documented the build. Better projects get higher multipliers, and there are bonuses for projects that get real users, contributors, downloads, or adoption outside LOADOUT.

*_Other planned stuff_*
Journals, seasons, track leaderboards, quests, sponsor drops, signal bonuses, and limited rewards.

*_AI_*
AI can help, but it shouldn't build the project for you. The current draft position is a maximum of 40% AI-generated implementation.

*_Timeline (will definitely change)_*
Now — feedback and website. Next — RSVP and a small test. After — sponsors and a pilot season. Later — full launch.

*_Shop suggestions_*
A good LOADOUT shop should contain stuff people actually want. If you want something added, post it in the Shop Suggestions tab; if someone already suggested it, just upvote it.

*_Feedback wanted_*
Are the tracks good? Would you care about levelling your Builder Profile? What would you actually save Cores for? Should levels unlock different rewards? What feels too similar to another YSWS?

Post ideas in #loadout 😺

Your projects build your LOADOUT.
```
